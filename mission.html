Our Mission

Rotationaries Foundation · 501(c)(3)

Rotationaries Foundation is a youth-serving 501(c)(3) educational nonprofit that supports FRC Team 9114 Rotationaries and related STEM programs in Fremont, CA. We provide hands-on robotics education, competition opportunities, mentorship, and community outreach that develop the next generation of engineers, innovators, and leaders.

01

Robotics Education

Students design, build, and program competition robots through FIRST Robotics, learning engineering, collaboration, and problem-solving in a hands-on environment.

02

Mentorship & Leadership

We give students real ownership of technical and operational roles so they grow into confident leaders who can teach, organize, and inspire others.

03

Community Outreach

We share STEM with our community through events, partnerships, and outreach that make robotics and engineering more accessible for everyone.
